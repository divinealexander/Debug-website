FAQ

Everything You Need To Know

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our support team.
What documents do I need to rent a car?

You will generally need a valid driver's license and a valid form of identification. Additional requirements may apply depending on the vehicle and rental conditions.

How old do I need to be to rent a car?

Rental age requirements may vary depending on the vehicle category. Please contact LEX-DRIVE before booking if you are unsure about the requirements.

Can I choose where to pick up the car?

Yes. During the booking process, you can enter your preferred pick-up and drop-off locations.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Cancellation policies depend on the booking conditions. Please review your rental agreement or contact our support team before cancelling.

Can I extend my rental period?

Rental extensions may be possible depending on vehicle availability. Contact LEX-DRIVE as early as possible if you need additional days.

What payment methods do you accept?

Available payment methods may include cards, bank transfers and payment at pickup. The available options will be displayed during the booking process.

Are the cars insured?

Insurance coverage depends on the rental agreement and vehicle. Please review the specific terms associated with your booking.

How can I contact LEX-DRIVE?

You can reach our team through the Contact page, where you can find our available contact details and send us a message.
